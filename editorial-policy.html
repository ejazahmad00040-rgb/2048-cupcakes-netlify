Editorial Policy

How we create, review, and update guides for this free cupcake puzzle

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Purpose of this policy

This Editorial Policy explains how content on 2048 Cupcakes is planned, written, reviewed, and updated. Our goal is helpful, transparent player information that supports a clean, trustworthy free game experience — including pages that may appear alongside advertising.

Primary content lives on the home page guide, How to Play, About, and related help/legal pages.

2. What we publish

We publish:

  • Gameplay rules, controls, and scoring explanations
  • Cupcake tier / flavor ladders and Kcal values used inside the game
  • Strategy tips, FAQs, and mobile/desktop how-tos
  • Site policies (privacy, cookies, ads, terms, disclaimer, DMCA)
  • Product and contact information for this independent fan project

We do not publish adult content, hate content, deceptive “get rich” claims, medical cures, or misleading download/malware pages. We do not sell fake “mods,” cracked APKs, or paid unlock keys for this browser game.

3. Accuracy and sourcing

Game mechanics descriptions are based on the live experience on this site. When we reference the original 2048 concept, we credit Gabriele Cirulli and link to reputable sources. Strategy tips are experiential guidance, not guaranteed paths to a specific score.

If a feature changes (for example undo limits, daily challenge rules, or sound settings), we update the relevant guide section when we become aware of the change. “Last updated” dates on policy pages reflect material revisions.

4. Original writing and uniqueness

Guides and policy pages are written for this project. We avoid copying competitor pages wholesale. Short quotes for attribution (for example license references) are clearly linked. Images of cupcake tiles are part of the game’s visual set and are described accurately for players.

5. Tone and clarity

We write in plain English aimed at casual players on phone and desktop. We avoid keyword stuffing, fake urgency, and clickbait that does not match the page. Flavor names and playful “Kcal” scoring stay clearly framed as game theme — never as real nutrition science. See our Disclaimer.

6. Advertising separation

Editorial explanations of how to play are independent from any ad placements. When ads are enabled, they follow our Advertisement Policy. We do not let advertisers rewrite gameplay rules or bury required disclosures.

7. User feedback and corrections

If you spot an error in a guide, broken control description, or outdated tip, tell us via the Contact page. Valid corrections are prioritized for the next content pass. Feedback does not create an employment, partnership, or paid-review relationship.

8. Conflicts of interest

2048 Cupcakes is a free fan-made browser game. We may use analytics or advertising to support hosting and maintenance. We disclose advertising practices on the Advertisement Policy and data practices on the Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.

9. Children and sensitive topics

Content is suitable for a general audience focused on a puzzle game. We do not target children with deceptive messaging. Parents should supervise younger users online. We avoid graphic violence, adult sexual content, and other categories restricted by major ad platforms’ publisher policies.

10. Updates to this policy

We may revise this Editorial Policy as the site grows. Continued use of the site after updates means you should review the latest version here. Related rules appear in our Terms & Conditions.
