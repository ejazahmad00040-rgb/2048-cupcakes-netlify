DMCA Policy

How we handle copyright infringement notices for this fan-made puzzle site

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Respect for intellectual property

2048 Cupcakes respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects visitors to do the same. Gameplay is inspired by the classic 2048 puzzle by Gabriele Cirulli under the MIT License. Our cupcake theme, writing, and site presentation are an independent fan-made project. Learn more on About and in our Terms & Conditions.

This DMCA Policy describes how copyright owners can notify us about allegedly infringing material hosted on this website.

2. What this policy covers

This process applies to material we host on 2048 Cupcakes — for example text, images, or downloadable assets on our pages. It does not control ads injected by third-party networks; report those through the network’s tools and see our Advertisement Policy.

3. Filing a DMCA takedown notice

If you believe content on this site infringes your copyright, send a written notice that includes all of the following (as required by 17 U.S.C. §512(c)(3)):

  1. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on their behalf
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (or a representative list if multiple works)
  3. Identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing, with enough detail for us to locate it (URL required)
  4. Your contact information (name, postal address, telephone number, and email address)
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law
  6. A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner

Submit notices through our Contact page (email draft / GitHub issue). Incomplete notices may delay review.

4. What we do after receiving a valid notice

Upon receiving a complete notice, we will review it promptly. Where appropriate, we may remove or disable access to the allegedly infringing material and document the action. We may notify the user who provided the material if applicable (this site is largely first-party static content).

5. Counter-notification

If your material was removed and you believe it was a mistake or misidentification, you may send a counter-notification including:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature
  2. Identification of the material removed and where it appeared before removal
  3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification
  4. Your name, address, and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your address (or any district where we may be found if outside the U.S.), and that you will accept service of process from the original complainant

Upon a valid counter-notice, we may restore material unless the original complainant seeks a court order as provided by law.

6. Repeat infringement

We may restrict access for parties who repeatedly submit infringing material or clearly abusive notices, consistent with applicable law and our Terms.

7. Misrepresentation

Submitting a knowingly false DMCA notice or counter-notice may create legal liability for damages, including costs and attorneys’ fees, under U.S. law. Only file notices you are entitled to file.

8. Trademarks and other rights

For trademark concerns unrelated to copyrightable content, contact us with details of the mark, registration (if any), and the specific URL. Fan-made disclaimers appear in our Disclaimer: we are not affiliated with bakery brands.

9. Attribution requests

If you believe we used your openly licensed work without proper credit, contact us. Many issues can be fixed with attribution or replacement without a formal DMCA process.

10. Policy updates

We may update this DMCA Policy as needed. Continued use of the site after changes means you should review the latest version. Related reading: Privacy Policy, Editorial Policy.
