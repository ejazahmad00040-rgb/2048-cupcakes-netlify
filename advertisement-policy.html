Advertisement Policy

Transparency about ads that may appear on this free puzzle site

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Why this policy exists

2048 Cupcakes is a free browser game. Hosting, maintenance, and content updates cost time and infrastructure. To keep the game free to play online, we may display advertisements through networks such as Google AdSense when the account and placements are approved and enabled.

This policy explains what players can expect. It works together with our Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, Editorial Policy, and Terms & Conditions.

2. Types of ads you may see

Depending on approval status, device, location, and inventory, you may see:

  • Display ads (banners or responsive units)
  • In-article or in-feed style units on guide sections
  • Contextual ads related to page topics (puzzle games, casual gaming)
  • Personalized ads based on partner cookies/IDs where allowed

We aim to place ads so they do not cover the game board controls or trick you into accidental clicks. The playable grid and primary “Play” actions remain user-initiated.

3. Publisher standards we follow

We design pages to align with major advertising partner rules for publishers, including expectations around:

  • Valuable, original content about a real product (this game and its guides)
  • Clear navigation and working pages (Home, How to Play, About, Contact, policies)
  • No deceptive layouts that imitate system dialogs or force misclicks
  • No prohibited content categories (for example adult sexual content, hate, violent extremism, or deceptive malware distribution)
  • Honest labeling of the experience as a free online puzzle — not a download storefront scam

Our About page and How to Play guide exist to help players, not to create thin doorway pages.

4. What we do not promote

We do not intentionally place site content that promotes illegal activity, counterfeit goods, weapons instructions, or other categories barred by Google Publisher Policies. Ad creatives themselves are served by third-party networks; we do not write every ad. If you see an inappropriate ad, you can often report it through the ad’s UI, and you can also contact us with a screenshot and approximate time.

5. Separation from editorial content

Gameplay instructions, tier tables, and strategy tips are editorial. Ads do not rewrite those rules. Sponsored labels will be used if we ever publish paid placements beyond standard network ads.

6. Data used for advertising

Ad partners may process IP address, cookie IDs, device information, and page URLs to serve and measure ads. We do not sell your game saves. Details are in the Cookie Policy and Privacy Policy.

7. Your choices

You may:

  • Use browser controls to block third-party cookies
  • Adjust Google ad personalization settings where available
  • Use device-level ad ID resets / limits on mobile OS settings
  • Continue playing with possible non-personalized or contextual ads

Blocking ads or cookies may affect site metrics and, in rare cases, layout spacing — but the core puzzle should remain playable.

8. Affiliates and sponsored posts

If we add affiliate links or sponsored articles in the future, we will disclose that relationship clearly on the relevant page and keep this policy updated.

9. Changes

Ad technology and partner requirements change. We may revise this Advertisement Policy and the “Last updated” date when material changes occur.

10. Contact

Advertising or policy questions: Contact. For copyright of creatives you own that appear incorrectly, see our DMCA Policy (for site content claims) or the ad network’s report tools (for network-served creatives).
