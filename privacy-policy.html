Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Overview

2048 Cupcakes is a free browser game. We do not run accounts and we do not collect personal information on our servers (this site is static).

Local Storage Data

The game stores the following in your browser's localStorage:

  • Game state (board, scores, win/loss status)
  • Best cupcake tier and best Kcal scores
  • Daily challenge / top scores on this device
  • Achievement progress and settings preferences
  • Sound and display settings

This data stays on your device. Clear it anytime via Settings → Clear saved data, or through your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

If Google Analytics (GA4) is enabled, Google may collect usage data such as page views, device/browser type, and approximate location. We configure Analytics with IP anonymization where available. Google’s policies apply to that data: Google Privacy Policy.

We do not run ads on this site. Optional share features may open your device's share sheet or a social network share page — those platforms have their own policies.

Google Search Console may be used to monitor indexing; verification does not collect personal gameplay data.
