Privacy Policy

How 2048 Cupcakes handles information when you play this free browser puzzle

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Who we are

2048 Cupcakes (“we,” “us”) operates the free online merge puzzle at this website. Learn more on our About Us page. You can play the game, read How to Play, or reach us via Contact.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you visit, what stays only on your device, and how third-party tools (analytics and optional advertising) may work. Related documents: Cookie Policy, Advertisement Policy, Terms & Conditions, and Disclaimer.

2. A static game — no player accounts

We do not require registration. There is no username/password system and we do not operate a player profile database on our own servers for scores. The site is delivered as static files (hosted on Netlify). Game progress is designed to live in your browser unless you clear it.

3. Information stored on your device

To make gameplay work, we store data in your browser’s localStorage (and similar storage), which may include:

  • Game board state, current and best scores, win/loss status
  • Best cupcake tier and best Kcal on that browser
  • Daily challenge / streak-style progress on this device
  • Achievement progress and UI preferences
  • Sound and music toggle settings

This information stays on your device. Clear it anytime via in-game Settings → Clear saved data (when available) or by clearing site data in your browser settings. Losing this data does not delete information held by third-party analytics or ad partners on their systems.

4. Information collected through analytics

When Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is enabled, Google may collect usage data such as page views, approximate geographic region, device and browser type, referral source, and similar diagnostics. We configure IP anonymization where available. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy.

We use analytics to understand whether the game and guides load correctly and which help pages (for example How to Play) are useful — not to sell a list of individual players.

5. Advertising partners

We may display ads through networks such as Google AdSense when enabled and approved. Those partners can use cookies and advertising identifiers as described in our Cookie Policy and Advertisement Policy. We do not intentionally collect your government ID, payment card number, or precise GPS coordinates for advertising.

6. Contact form and email

If you use the Contact form, your name, email address, and message are handled by your own email client (mailto draft) or by whatever channel you choose (for example GitHub Issues). We use that information only to respond to your request.

7. How we use information

Information is used to:

  • Operate and improve the puzzle and site performance
  • Remember settings and progress on your device
  • Measure traffic and fix broken pages
  • Support free access through compliant advertising when enabled
  • Respond to messages you send us
  • Protect the site against abuse and comply with law

8. Legal bases (where applicable)

Depending on your region, we rely on one or more of: providing the service you request (playing a browser game), legitimate interests in securing and improving a free site, consent for non-essential cookies/ads where required, and legal obligations when we must respond to valid requests.

9. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data with processors/partners only as needed to host the site, measure usage, serve ads, or comply with law — for example Netlify (hosting), Google (Analytics / AdSense when used), and font or CDN providers. Optional share buttons open third-party share sheets; those platforms have their own policies.

10. Retention

Local game data remains until you clear it. Analytics and advertising retention follow each partner’s policies. Contact emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request.

11. Security

We use HTTPS hosting and limit what we store server-side. No method of internet transmission is 100% secure. Protect your own device and avoid sharing screenshots that contain private information you do not want public.

12. Children’s privacy

The game is a general-audience puzzle. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for marketing profiles. Parents and guardians should supervise younger users. If you believe a child provided personal information to us directly, contact us and we will delete it where applicable.

13. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal data, or to object to certain processing. Because most gameplay data never leaves your browser, many requests are resolved by clearing local site data. For analytics/ads, use browser controls and partner opt-outs described in the Cookie Policy. To contact us about privacy, use the Contact page.

14. International visitors

Our hosting and partners may process data in the United States or other countries. If you visit from another region, you understand that information may be transferred to countries with different data-protection laws, subject to applicable safeguards those providers offer.

15. Search Console and SEO tools

We may use Google Search Console or similar tools to monitor indexing. Verification tags do not collect your gameplay saves.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when features or partners change. The “Last updated” date will change when revisions are material. Continued use of the site after an update means you should review the new version.

17. Contact

Privacy questions: Contact. For copyright of hosted assets, see our DMCA Policy. Content standards: Editorial Policy.
