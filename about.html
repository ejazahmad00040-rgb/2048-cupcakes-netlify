About 2048 Cupcakes

A sweet, mobile-friendly twist on the classic sliding puzzle

What this is

2048 Cupcakes is a free browser game: slide matching cupcakes together, climb flavor tiers, and chase a high Kcal score. We rebuilt the experience with polish you actually feel — sound, undo, celebrations, daily challenges, and friend challenge links — with no account and no download.

What you get

  • Sound & music — Toggle merge sounds and a light background beat
  • Undo — Up to 20 steps when a move goes wrong
  • Achievements — Badges for big merges and milestones
  • Juice — Confetti, combos, and tier-up moments
  • Daily & friend challenges — Same-day board + shareable beat-my-score links
  • Works everywhere — Keyboard on desktop, swipe on phone

Credits

Gameplay is inspired by 2048 by Gabriele Cirulli (MIT License). This site is an independent fan-made cupcake-themed version and is not affiliated with any bakery brand.

Play free, anywhere
