Cookie Policy

How this free browser game uses cookies, local storage, and similar technologies

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Overview

This Cookie Policy explains how 2048 Cupcakes uses cookies, local storage, pixels, and similar technologies when you visit our site. It should be read with our Privacy Policy, Advertisement Policy, and Terms & Conditions.

The game itself is a static browser experience: your board and settings primarily stay on your device. Analytics and advertising technologies (when enabled) may set cookies controlled by third parties such as Google.

2. What are cookies and local storage?

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. They can be session-based or persistent. Local storage is a browser feature that can keep larger game state on your device until you clear it.

Similar technologies include pixels, tags, and software development kits used by analytics or ad partners to measure visits or show ads.

3. Essential / functional storage we use

To make the puzzle work, we store gameplay data in your browser (typically via localStorage), including:

  • Current board, scores, win/loss status
  • Best cupcake tier and best Kcal on this device
  • Daily challenge progress and achievement state
  • Sound, music, and display preferences

These items are required for basic functionality and remembering your progress on the same browser. You can clear them with in-game Settings → Clear saved data (when available) or by clearing site data in your browser.

4. Analytics cookies

When Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is enabled, Google may set cookies or use similar identifiers to collect aggregated usage information such as page views, approximate location, device/browser type, and referral source. We configure IP anonymization where the product supports it.

Google’s processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Analytics helps us understand which guides (for example How to Play) are useful and whether the game loads correctly on mobile.

5. Advertising cookies

If Google AdSense or similar networks are active on the site, those partners may use cookies and advertising IDs to:

  • Serve and measure ads
  • Limit how often you see the same ad
  • Provide aggregated reporting to publishers
  • Support interest-based advertising where permitted by law and your settings

Details about what we allow on-site appear in our Advertisement Policy. Ad partners are independent controllers of their cookies.

6. Preference and performance technologies

We may load third-party fonts (for example Google Fonts) or hosting-related assets that can involve network requests. These are used for design performance and branding consistency with our pink cupcake theme — not to sell your personal profile.

7. How to control cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings (block, delete, or alert on cookies). Most browsers let you clear local storage and site data for this domain. Note that blocking storage may reset your game progress and some preferences.

Where required by law, you may also use Google’s ad settings or industry opt-out tools for interest-based ads. Opting out of personalized ads does not always mean you will see zero ads — you may still see contextual or non-personalized ads.

8. Do Not Track and consent signals

Browser “Do Not Track” signals are not consistently standardized. We honor applicable legal requirements for consent and disclosure in regions where they apply. If we add a dedicated consent banner later, this policy will be updated to match.

9. Children

We do not knowingly use cookies to build marketing profiles of children. Guardians should use browser parental controls where appropriate. See also the Privacy Policy.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this Cookie Policy when our technologies change. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the latest material revision. Questions: Contact.
